GLYPH SCRAMBLER

A digitally fossilized cabinet of visual Unicode curiosities.

SCRAMBLE THE MIND

Enter ordinary text and transform its letters into randomized visual glyphs. Matching multi-letter ligatures may appear where available, while the spacing rule keeps nearby letter glyphs from repeating the same Unicode block.

OUTPUT
⚠ PLEASE READ BEFORE COPYING ⚠

This is decorative text, not readable text. These look-alike glyphs are different Unicode characters. Software, search engines, and screen readers may treat or pronounce them unpredictably, and some devices may not display them. Keep names, instructions, accessibility-critical text, and important information in ordinary readable characters.

Rule: no two non-symbol glyphs from the same Unicode block are placed within any three-letter window. Multi-letter ligatures are exempt.
